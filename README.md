# Free Standalone Image Aspect Ratio & Canvas Padder (.exe)

A fast, lightweight, and 100% offline Windows desktop application designed to pad product photography and design assets to uniform aspect ratios (1:1, 4:3, 16:9) without cropping, stretching, or losing quality.

## 🚀 Key Features
- **Zero Cropping:** Intelligently adds padding to fit target aspect ratios without cutting off product edges.
- **Batch Processing:** Select individual files or entire folders for simultaneous conversion.
- **Flexible Fills:** Choose solid background colors or smooth blurred background expansions.
- **Multi-Format Export:** Save output files in JPG, PNG, or WebP formats.
- **100% Offline & Secure:** Keep product catalogs and proprietary assets safe on your local PC.

## 📥 How to Download & Run
1. Go to the **[Releases](../../releases)** tab on the right side of this repository page.
2. Download the latest `canvas_padder.exe` file.
3. Launch `canvas_padder.exe` on your Windows PC, select your images, configure your canvas settings, and click **Pad & Uniform Image Canvas**!
